Typed Artifacts, Structured Worlds, and Effectful AI

Mateo Petel

What does it mean for an AI-generated artifact to become part of a world of knowledge, software, science, or action?

These essays examine the conditions under which generated artifacts are valid, reusable, structurally consistent, and fit for consequential use.
