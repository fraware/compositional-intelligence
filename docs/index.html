Compositional
Intelligence

Typed Artifacts, Structured Worlds, and Effectful AI

Mateo Petel

Compositional Intelligence proposes that generative AI should be evaluated through typed artifacts and the structured worlds those artifacts enter.

Generated objects should be judged by the worlds they can enter, the structures they preserve, the warrant that supports them, the uses they permit, and the consequences they can produce under controlled authority.
