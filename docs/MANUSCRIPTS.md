# Compositional Intelligence — manuscript editions

Mateo Petel

## Publication status

**Public working drafts.** The materials in this directory are released for reading, discussion, and scholarly criticism. They are not represented as peer reviewed, formally certified, experimentally validated, or the final approved edition.

Two historical editions are preserved as separate directories.

- [`manuscripts/v0.3-original/`](../manuscripts/v0.3-original/) contains the **nine original V3 Word documents**, preserved byte-for-byte.
- [`manuscripts/v0.4-candidate/`](../manuscripts/v0.4-candidate/) contains the **nine M3B scientific-review Markdown candidates**. These incorporate some proposed mathematical and citation-scope corrections. They have not been reconciled into a final author-approved edition. These are not a Word-to-Markdown conversion of the V3 documents.

A versioned release manifest under [`manuscripts/SHA256.json`](../manuscripts/SHA256.json) records file sizes and SHA-256 digests. Do not silently merge these two editions or label either as an independently certified final publication.

## Scientific boundaries

The series proposes a framework for typed artifacts, structured transformations, linguistic composition, architecture, quantitative evidence, and consequential agent actions. Several formal definitions and methodological claims remain under active review. The associated benchmark work consists of designed finite reference fixtures and exploratory infrastructure, not established AI-system performance gains. Citation-group review has been provisional; claims of exhaustive independent source certification are unwarranted.

The public manuscript release excludes private review correspondence, internal decision ledgers, unapproved provenance artifacts, and experimental milestone archives. Those records remain in a separately maintained research backup until disclosure is independently reviewed.
