# Typed Generativity — Full evaluation protocol from Scientific Revision V4

This document retains the detailed Section 7.4 of V4 as an editorial companion to the final publication manuscript. It is a research *proposal*, not an executed study or preregistered design. The final manuscript summarizes its essential safeguards. The implementation artifacts and acceptance handbook retain their separate status.

<h3 id="identification-and-analysis-protocol">7.4 Identification and analysis protocol</h3>
<p><strong>Interventions and comparison.</strong> The principal randomized contrast assigns type-first articulation (T) or neutral deliberation (L). Both receive identical task information, allowed retrieval, tool permissions, model revision, output cap, and turn budget. Both have a preparatory phase and an identically specified final-artifact slot. T directs that phase toward target-kind identification, constraints, ambiguity, and validation; L directs unconstrained consideration of the task without those prescribed components. Spontaneous target extraction in L is part of the comparator, not a violation. The complete instruction packages are the treatments; this contrast alone does not isolate any individual field of T. Direct response (D) is descriptive. Oracle specifications introduce privileged information, while constrained decoding and checker-assisted repair introduce additional mechanisms; their diagnostic contrasts remain separate and domain-eligible.</p>
<p><strong>Outcomes and eligibility.</strong> Authors establish admissible target interpretations, mandatory acceptance criteria, evidence grades, available validation methods, and a frozen rule for interpreting indeterminate judgments before any confirmatory generation. The primary endpoint refers to compliance with that domain-specific contract. It does not pool proof validity, prospective protocol quality, and authorized tool execution into an undifferentiated scientific claim. For completed artifacts, every mandatory criterion must have a supported positive final judgment. A rejected mandatory criterion makes the artifact unsuccessful; absent or inconclusive mandatory assessments leave the artifact's acceptance unknown, except under a separately declared conservative scoring convention. Output refusals and model-side failures are retained under assigned treatment, with explicit outcome rules for the one-shot artifact cohort. Infrastructure failures are retained in the assignment ledger yet are treated as missing outcomes rather than model errors. Clarification quality and interactive execution form a separate cohort.</p>
<p><strong>Fixed-corpus estimand.</strong> For eligible task i, condition c, and randomized repetition s, let Y<sub>i,c,s</sub> be the prespecified binary acceptance outcome. Write μ<sub>i,c</sub> for its expectation over the defined generation and evaluation randomness. For a fixed domain-specific task set I<sub>d</sub>, the target contrast is</p>
<p class="schema-equation"><code>Δ<sub>d</sub><sup>fixed</sup> = (1 / |I<sub>d</sub>|) Σ<sub>i∈I<sub>d</sub></sub> (μ<sub>i,T</sub> − μ<sub>i,L</sub>).</code></p>
<p>With R preallocated trials per task and condition, estimate each μ by its trial mean and Δ by the mean of task-level contrasts. Preserve every assigned run in the denominator; a generated-artifact-only analysis conditions on a treatment-affected event. Numeric seeds across different prompts do not establish common random numbers, and deterministic APIs do not necessarily produce independent draws. For an agreed fixed corpus, resampling individual outputs does not quantify uncertainty over task selection. Task-family dependence matters for claims about a broader task population. The primary report distinguishes repeated-trial Monte Carlo uncertainty, descriptive variability across fixed tasks, and any justified population-sampling uncertainty, without representing one as the other.</p>
<p><strong>Assignment, isolation, and resource accounting.</strong> Preallocate trial identifiers and condition assignments before observing responses. Randomize order within task-family or execution blocks, start each run in a fresh conversation and isolated state, and reset mutable tools to a declared snapshot or record nonreplayable changes. Pin instructions, model revision, sampling parameters, retrieval corpus, tool permissions, and validator versions. Record predeclared maximum input-output limits, realized tokens, elapsed time, tool actions, retries, and cost. Equal caps are a resource-policy match, not an assertion of equal realized costs. Allocation follows assignment even if the model ignores the instruction. Exclusions for articulation compliance, successful extraction, or completed validation would be post-assignment selections. If model versions or tools change mid-study, distinguish predeclared blocks from unplanned protocol deviations.</p>
<p><strong>Assessment independence.</strong> A frozen, treatment-independent parser extracts the final-artifact slot. Reviewers see a randomized artifact identifier, the scoring task, and the preapproved rubric, without the preparatory trace or condition label. Artifact wording might nevertheless reveal the assignment, so assessor blinding is an attempted safeguard rather than a guarantee. Separate the generation operator from the validator operator. Deterministic validators run in pinned environments; nonmechanical criteria receive two initially independent reviewers, preserved raw decisions, and an adjudication procedure. Report initial disagreement, unresolved status, and evidence for final judgments. No confirmatory output is used to alter task admissibility, scoring rules, or extraction code.</p>
<p><strong>Analysis and statistical claims.</strong> The main result reports each domain's assigned-trial acceptance contrast, with per-task means, execution-status counts, and resource use. Any cross-domain summary is an explicitly chosen macro-average over the six domains, with equal weights stated as a normative weighting convention rather than an estimate for an unspecified real-world task mix. Predeclare the estimand, interval method, clustering assumptions, and multiplicity treatment. Missing outcomes need bounds and a separate scenario analysis, not silent removal. A conservative rule treating unknown artifact judgments as failures describes a scoring policy; it is distinct from assuming infrastructure failures to be failed model responses. Pilot items diagnose feasibility. Confirmatory sample sizes depend on the smallest meaningful effect, expected discordance, generation variability, source-family dependence, and annotation budget.</p>
<p><strong>Contamination and provenance.</strong> Record task origin, public-release status, generation date, retained source fingerprints where permitted, near-duplicate checks, retrieval logs, and model-exposure uncertainty. Retrieval-time contamination deserves separate attention: search or tool results containing an evaluation task or its reference response can disclose an answer even if it was absent from training. Freeze or archive searchable corpora and explicitly audit retrieval transcripts for reference-answer exposure. A clean textual overlap scan establishes neither the absence of semantic overlap nor private training contamination. Preserve separate development and confirmatory corpora; new exposure changes the interpretation of later reuse. Scientific protocol reviews and tool-use simulations retain evidence labels indicating what was actually executed.</p>
<p><strong>Scope of identification.</strong> A measured T-versus-L difference identifies the conditional effect of assigning one complete instruction package over the other under the tested conditions, provided assignment, compliance with the evaluation protocol, and measurement assumptions hold. It does not identify the causal contribution of the target-kind label, the specification record, or an internal type representation in isolation. The recorded target description is post-assignment, and its association with acceptance is not a mediation estimate. Further ablations need fixed task information, comparable preparation slots, and separately randomized variations of the target-specification components. Claims about latent representations need causally interpretable internal interventions beyond output prompting alone.</p>
